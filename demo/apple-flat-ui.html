Attention is a scarce resource

ESSAY · 8 MIN

Most people treat focus as a mood. It is closer to a budget. Once you spend it on shallow tabs, little remains for deep work.

The hard part is not motivation. It is protecting a few uninterrupted blocks every week, and refusing to negotiate them away.

Words like overwhelm show up when the budget is already gone. Naming the feeling helps, but the fix is structural.
